# YouTube upload instructions

Upload only the final, reviewed recording. This repository does not contain a
video or a public URL.

1. Record the ≤180-second sequence in [demo-script.md](demo-script.md). Keep
   the on-screen `MOCK`, `Local Ollama`, and `PayPal Sandbox` labels visible.
2. Review the complete export frame by frame for client secrets, OAuth tokens,
   session cookies, buyer identity, emails, full provider/merchant IDs, browser
   extensions, local paths, and unrelated notifications. Redact before upload.
3. Upload to the owner-controlled YouTube account. Use an accurate English
   title and description; do not imply production hosting, real-money payment,
   product discovery, or a verified capture that the footage does not show.
4. Set visibility required by the official hackathon rules and verify the final
   URL while signed out or in a private browser window. Confirm captions, sound,
   and final runtime.
5. Paste the verified public URL here and in Devpost only after verification:

   `PUBLIC_YOUTUBE_URL: <owner to insert after verified upload>`

6. Retain the unredacted source recording only in the approved private location;
   do not add it to Git. Record upload timestamp and reviewer in the submission
   receipt record.
